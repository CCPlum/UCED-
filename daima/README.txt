S1 represents the scenario considering only intra‑provincial multi‑energy complementary collaborative optimization. S2 corresponds to the collaborative optimization of local on‑site consumption of provincial loads and cross‑regional power delivery. S3 denotes inter‑provincial resource collaborative optimization, and S4 stands for trans‑provincial and cross‑regional collaborative optimization.
For preliminary model case settings, real‑world raw data are not adopted. Only the characteristic profiles of wind‑solar power and load are simulated, covering intra‑daily and intra‑weekly features. Eight thermal power units are considered with aggregation treatment. Given the limited dataset, no separate parameter input table is generated.
For the generation dispatch optimization of units in Xinjiang, case studies are preliminarily carried out based on real‑world data. Annual datasets are applied for week‑scale rolling optimization, while specific power profiles for outward power delivery are not yet incorporated in the current version.
